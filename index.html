home-agent

A private home-automation tool for one household.

home-agent runs on the owner's own computer at home. It helps one family keep track of everyday paperwork: it reads order, bank and school notices that arrive in the owner's own Gmail, files them in a private archive on that computer, and adds an appointment to the owner's calendar when the owner confirms it.

It is not a public service. It has one user, the owner, who signs in with his own Google account. Nobody else can sign up or use it.
